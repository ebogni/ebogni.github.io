VikingZone Promo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

VikingZone Promo (https://ebogni.github.io/promo/) is a small tool made by Emanuel Bogni (VikingZone, San Juan, Argentina) to upload short gameplay clips of our own games to our own YouTube channel. It is used only by its owner.

YouTube API Services

VikingZone Promo uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • When you press "Connect with YouTube", Google asks you to grant the scope youtube.upload. It only allows the tool to upload videos to your channel.
  • The tool receives a temporary access token from Google. It lives only in your browser's memory while the page is open. It is never sent to our servers (we have none) and is never stored.
  • The video file, title, description and tags you choose are sent directly from your browser to YouTube.

What we store or share

Nothing. We do not store, sell or share any user data. The page has no analytics, no cookies and no ads.

How to revoke access

Press "Disconnect" in the tool, or remove access at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
